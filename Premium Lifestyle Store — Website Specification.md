# PREMIUM LIFESTYLE STORE
## Shopify-Style E-Commerce Website Specification

---

# 01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a premium, modern e-commerce website for a business that **directly imports brand-new products from China**.

The store will sell affordable, useful, attractive and trending products for everyday life.

This is NOT a used-product marketplace.

### Important

All products are **BRAND NEW**.

Do NOT use:

- Used
- Second-hand
- Refurbished
- Pre-owned
- Previously owned
- Condition grades such as "Good / Fair / Excellent"

The website should communicate:

**NEW PRODUCTS • DIRECT IMPORT • SMART PRICES**

---

The business sources products directly from China and sells them locally.

The website should have a **premium Shopify storefront aesthetic**.

Visual references:

- Modern lifestyle brands

# 48. DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES

## IMPORTANT

The website should look **expensive**, while the products remain affordable.

Prioritize:

1. Product photography
2. Typography
3. Whitespace
4. Product cards
5. Navigation
6. Mobile shopping experience
7. Conversion

Do not overload the website with effects.

---

# 49. WHAT THE WEBSITE SHOULD FEEL LIKE

When someone opens the website, they should immediately think:

> "This is a premium store with really interesting products."

Not:

> "This is a cheap Chinese products website."

The products may be affordable, but the **brand presentation should be premium**.
